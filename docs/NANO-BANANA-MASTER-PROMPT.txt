──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STYLE CONTRACT  —  ισχύει για ΚΑΘΕ εικονίδιο, μην το αλλάξεις
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Create a single food-menu icon in this exact house style:

DRAWING STYLE
· Hand-drawn illustration, as if inked with a fine 0.3mm technical pen
  and then filled with soft watercolour washes.
· Outline: fine, confident, slightly uneven dark-brown ink line
  (#3A2A1E), consistent 2–3px weight at 144px, never pure black.
· Fill: soft flat watercolour, gently uneven, with a little pigment
  pooling near the outline. Not vector-flat, not gradient-heavy.
· Shading: light diagonal cross-hatch only where a shadow would fall.
  No airbrush, no gloss, no 3D render, no photorealism.
· Palette: warm muted earthy tones only — terracotta #B4623A,
  olive green #38564F, cream #F5F0E3, gold #A07E3C, soft brown #7A5C22.
  Muted and appetising, never neon, never saturated primary colours.

COMPOSITION
· ONE single centred subject. Nothing else in frame.
· Even breathing room on all four sides — roughly 10% padding.
· Slight 3/4 viewing angle, as if looking down at a table.
· Must stay instantly readable when shrunk to 40×40 pixels:
  bold silhouette, few details, generous internal spacing.

ABSOLUTELY NOT
· No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature, no watermark.
· No plate under the food unless the item IS a bowl or a plate.
· No table, no surface, no ground plane, no cast shadow.
· No frame, no border, no circle badge, no container.
· No drop shadow, no glow, no vignette, no reflection.

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  TRANSPARENCY  —  ΤΟ ΠΙΟ ΚΡΙΣΙΜΟ ΜΕΡΟΣ                    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
· Output MUST be a PNG-24 with a real ALPHA CHANNEL.
· The background must be 100% transparent — zero pixels of colour,
  zero white, zero off-white, zero paper texture, zero gradient.
· The alpha must be fully transparent right up to the ink outline.
· Anti-aliased edges are fine; a halo, fringe or soft white glow
  around the subject is NOT — no matte fringing of any kind.
· Nothing may bleed off the edges. The whole subject fits inside.

⚠ IF you cannot output a true alpha channel, then instead render the
  subject on a FLAT PURE MAGENTA background, hex exactly #FF00FF,
  perfectly uniform, no shading, no gradient, no glow, no reflection
  of magenta onto the subject — so the background can be keyed out
  cleanly afterwards. Never use white or grey as the fallback.

OUTPUT
· Square canvas, 144 × 144 pixels, unless the item says otherwise.
· One icon per image.
